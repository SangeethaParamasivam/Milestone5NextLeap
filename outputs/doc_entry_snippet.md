Fee issue: 

Explanation:
- ChatGPT and the API platform use different billing systems, so charges for each appear separately.  
- Billing for a ChatGPT web subscription is controlled in Settings → Billing.  
- Subscriptions may be purchased through the web, Apple App Store, or Google Play; using more than one channel can generate distinct charges.  
- A free‑trial payment can fail if the bank or card declines the transaction.  
- Payment failures can also occur due to incorrect billing details or because the account is in an unsupported country or region.
